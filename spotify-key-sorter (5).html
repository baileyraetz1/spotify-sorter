Spotify Key Sorter

Reads your playlist → detects musical key+mode → creates up to 24 sorted playlists

1 Spotify Developer Credentials

You need a free Spotify Developer app to use the API. Takes about 2 minutes to set up.

How to get your credentials:
1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard
2. Click your app → Settings
3. Under Redirect URIs, delete any old ones and add:
https://oauth.pstmn.io/v1/callback
4. Click Add then Save
5. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret below

Paste the full Spotify playlist URL or just the playlist ID.

Playlists will be named like "Prefix – C Major". Leave blank to use just the key name.

2 Complete Authentication

A Spotify login window should have opened. Authorize the app, then paste the full redirect URL you land on below.

After clicking "Agree" on Spotify, your browser lands on a Postman page. Copy the complete URL from the address bar — it will contain ?code= in it. Paste the whole thing here. Move fast — the code expires in ~60 seconds!
